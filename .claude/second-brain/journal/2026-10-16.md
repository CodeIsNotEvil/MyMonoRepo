# 2026-10-16

- Planned a new project, working name Druckwerk: MIT C# libraries on NuGet for barcodes, QR codes,
  labels, receipts and vector graphics, exporting SVG/PDF/PNG/JPEG/TIFF and printing on wired printers
  on Windows and Linux; later a server connector and a Blazor UI for many connectors ([[druckwerk]]).
- Nothing decided yet. The plan lists proposed decisions (licence policy, package split, pure managed
  core shared with the browser, raster-first printing) and seven open questions for the owner.
- Licences checked for the obvious candidates: ImageSharp/SixLabors.Fonts (Six Labors Split License)
  and QuestPDF (community licence) are not free for everyone, so they're out. SkiaSharp (MIT) is the
  only native dependency, and only in the raster/PDF package.
- nuget.org had no packages named `Druckwerk` or `CINE` on 2026-10-16 (checked through
  `api.nuget.org/v3-flatcontainer`; the search host `azuresearch-usnc.nuget.org` is blocked from the
  cloud sandbox).
- Owner's answers to the first round: MDR-conform labelling is required; every kind of printer
  connection, since customers' setups are unknown; fiscal receipts are in scope; print jobs may come
  from anywhere. Apps stay in the monorepo while 0.x and get their own repo at 1.0
  ([[0018-apps-get-their-own-repo-at-1-0]]).
- The plan now has: a managed renderer and PDF/A writer for byte-identical output on every OS (so MDR
  customers validate once; Skia only for JPEG/WebP), connection URIs with a transport for each kind
  (spooler, CUPS/IPP, raw TCP, LPD, serial, USB with and without a queue, parallel, Bluetooth Classic
  and BLE, connector forwarding), job sources for the connector (REST, 9100 listener, IPP endpoint, hot
  folders, webhooks, relay mode for NAT), `Druckwerk.Receipts` with fiscal profiles,
  `Druckwerk.Medical` for UDI, and a validation kit per release.
- New resource notes: [[mdr-labelling]] and [[fiscal-receipts]].
- Gotcha: the ISO 7000 / 15223-1 symbol artwork is ISO's copyright; don't ship it in the MIT packages
  until that's cleared.
