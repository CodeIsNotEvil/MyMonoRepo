GroceryTracker

Grocery spending, on your own server

Version {{ gt_version }} · what's new

Track what your household spends on groceries and who owes whom, without handing your receipts to anyone.

  • Works offline as an installable web app and syncs when it can.
  • Dashboard for this month, 3, 6 and 12 months, and year to date.
  • Split costs between people, with settle-up suggestions.
  • Import your old spreadsheet from CSV.
  • Runs in containers, even on a Raspberry Pi.
  • MIT licensed.

LaunchHeim

A Valheim mod launcher

Version {{ lh_version }} · what's new

Keep every modpack in its own instance and switch between them with one click. Your game install stays vanilla.

  • Each instance has its own BepInEx, mods and configs.
  • Mods from Thunderstore, Nexus Mods and CurseForge, with dependencies installed for you.
  • Updates for Thunderstore mods, and Nexus "Mod Manager Download" links open in it.
  • Share modpacks as files that r2modman reads too, and follow the game's logs live in a console.
  • Join your servers and open your worlds with the character and setup you played them with last time.
  • An Android companion (coming to F-Droid): pick mods on the go, see who's online, and sync the lists back to the PC.
  • For Linux (Arch, Debian/Ubuntu, Fedora/RHEL) and Windows 10/11.
  • MIT licensed.
